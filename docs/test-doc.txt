EduBridge System Documentation

Overview
EduBridge is a platform designed to connect students with verified education agents. 
The platform helps students find universities, apply for courses, schedule appointments with agents, 
and track their application progress.

Key Features
1. Student–Agent Matching  
   EduBridge automatically matches students with suitable agents based on country preferences, course interests, and academic profile.

2. Appointment Scheduling  
   Students can book appointments with agents. Agents can view, update, and manage appointments from their dashboard.

3. Course & University Selection  
   Students can browse courses, compare universities, and select their preferred options. 
   Agents get access to these selections to guide the student properly.

4. Application Tracking  
   Each student has a statusHistory field that shows:  
   - Document Collection  
   - Application Submission  
   - Offer Letter Received  
   - Visa Filing  
   - Pre-departure Preparation  

5. Communication System  
   The platform supports real-time messaging and automated email notifications for:
   - Appointment confirmations  
   - Status updates  
   - Required actions (missing documents, interview dates, etc.)

Important Definitions
• Agent: A verified counsellor who helps students apply to universities.  
• Student: A user seeking university admission guidance.  
• Appointment: A scheduled meeting between agent and student.  
• Course: A specific academic program that the student selects.  
• University: An institution that offers academic programs.  

FAQ
Q: Can students change their assigned agent?  
A: Yes. Students may request reassignment if they are not satisfied with their current agent.

Q: How do agents track progress?  
A: Through the statusHistory array attached to each student profile.

Q: Does EduBridge apply on behalf of the student?  
A: No. Agents handle the application, but students must provide all required documents.
